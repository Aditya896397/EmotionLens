**Method.** Test images were darkened as `I' = factor * I^gamma` (gamma > 1 crushes shadows). With enhancement 'on', images whose mean gray level fell below the low-light threshold (70.0) were processed with the pipeline's adaptive gamma + CLAHE. **Limitation.** Synthetic darkening does not reproduce real sensor noise, colour casts or motion blur in low light, so real-world degradation is likely larger.
