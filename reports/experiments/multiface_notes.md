**Method.** Synthetic frames were built by tiling N random FER-2013 test faces (upscaled to 144 px with replicated borders) and run through the same FrameProcessor the app uses. pipeline_ms covers detection, alignment, batched classification and smoothing, not camera capture or display. **Limitation.** Tiled, upscaled 48x48 crops are easier/harder than real scenes in different ways (no occlusion or pose variety, but low resolution), so recall here is indicative only.
